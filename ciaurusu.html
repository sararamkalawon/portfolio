Sara Ramkalawon presents

The course in question focused on the development of sustainable products so as to reduce waste. This led to the birth of Ciaurusu, a high-end brand, that tries to reduce plastic production by offering a solid shampoo bar made with natural ingredients from Sicily and that can easily adapt to any condition. Inside the project there other elements that connect to the sicilian heritage, like the name of the brand "Ciaurusu" that means "scented" in sicilian, or the characters that come from the famous sicilian card game
