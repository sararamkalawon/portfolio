From a simple assignment...

Linea nove was born from an uni assignment and it lead to my first ever font. The inspiration came randomly from the sign inside the public buses that I took everyday to go to uni. This resulted in this modular font made of two elements, a square and a quarter of circle, that put together create this wiggly effect. It's definitely not perfect, however it's something that I cherrish as it's my first ever attempt at making a font (something that I've always wanted to do)
